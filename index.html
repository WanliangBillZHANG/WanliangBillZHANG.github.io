01 / Research & current work

Developing better ways to represent
the atmosphere over complex cities.

Models that can
see the city.

I develop and evaluate numerical models for atmospheric transport and urban weather. My work focuses on how turbulence, buildings, terrain, and surface heterogeneity shape the boundary layer — and how those processes can be represented in weather and climate models.

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Urban Climate Design Lab (UCDL) at the National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Chao Yuan on large-scale numerical models for complex dynamic systems.

01

Urban boundary layer

Improving the representation of momentum and scalar transport over heterogeneous, built-up terrain.

02

Weather & climate models

WRF, MPAS, LES, and regional downscaling for urban forecasting and climate applications.

03

Turbulence & buildings

Coupling planetary boundary-layer schemes with building-effect parameterizations and urban canopy models.
